01 / VULNERABILITY INTELLIGENCE

CERBERUS

See the signal through the noise.

Unifies CISA KEV, EPSS, Shodan exposure data, and MITRE ATT&CK mapping. The demo follows a triage cycle that turns 249 CVEs into four prioritized actions with asset ownership and remediation context.

02 / CREDENTIAL RESPONSE

ACER

Faster response. Human judgment.

Detects leaked credentials through Recorded Future, queues exposures for analyst review, and triggers approved password resets through Microsoft Entra. The demo shows the workflow from detection to remediation.

03 / SECURITY OPERATIONS

ATLAS

The right work to the right people.

Classifies security review tickets by risk and complexity, routes them to available analysts, and reassigns work as capacity changes. The demo traces five tickets through intake, routing, and resolution.
