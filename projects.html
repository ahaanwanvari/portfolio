CERBERUS

Vulnerability Intelligence Platform

How it Works

CERBERUS automatically scans the NVD, CISA KEV database, and Shodan to identify vulnerable systems. It correlates 249+ CVEs into just 4 prioritized actions by analyzing EPSS scores, public exploits, and organizational exposure.

For high-priority vulnerabilities like Apache httpd with KEV status and ransomware exploits, the system generates a dossier showing exact hostnames, creates bulk assignments to engineering teams, and exports actionable remediation tickets.

The demo shows a complete triage cycle: scanning network data, collapsing CVEs into actions, identifying internet-facing assets, confirming ownership, and routing tickets to the right teams.

ACER

Automated Credential Exposure Response

How it Works

ACER continuously monitors Recorded Future's dark-web intelligence for leaked credentials belonging to your organization. When exposures are detected, the system automatically pulls credential metadata and risk scoring.

A human-in-the-loop approval queue prioritizes high-risk exposures for analyst review. Approved resets trigger Microsoft Entra password reset APIs for immediate account protection. Denied cases get logged with reasoning for compliance.

The demo shows 3 leaked credentials being detected, triaged by risk level, approved/denied by analysts, and automatically remediated with password resets — turning a 48-hour incident response into a 15-minute automated workflow.

ATLAS

Automated Ticket Leveling & Assignment System

How it Works

ATLAS ingests security review tickets and automatically classifies them into tiers (S, M, L, XL) based on risk scoring, merchant type, and fraud indicators. Ambiguous low-scores get escalated to senior analysts for dual review.

The system uses round-robin and smart load-balancing to assign tickets to available analysts, respecting workload limits and skill levels. When analysts go on PTO, their tickets automatically return to the queue and get reassigned.

The demo shows 5 tickets ingested, classified into risk tiers, routed to the right analysts, one analyst going on PTO (triggering re-routing), and all tickets resolved — demonstrating continuous workflow automation.
